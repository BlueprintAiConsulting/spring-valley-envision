### About BlueprintEnvision
BlueprintEnvision is an AI-powered visualizer and material takeoff platform engineered specifically for roofing, siding, and exterior home remodeling contractors. 

Contractors currently lose high-margin siding and roofing deals because homeowners struggle to envision how new colors, profiles, and trim will look on their actual home. BlueprintEnvision solves this by allowing contractors and homeowners to upload a photo of any house and instantly preview photorealistic CertainTeed siding profiles, shutter colors, trim packages, and accents in seconds.

### Why This is an Exceptional Acquisition:
1. **High-Ticket B2B Audience:** Home remodeling and exterior contractors have high customer lifetime values ($15k–$40k average siding job) and willingly pay $99–$249/mo for sales enablement software.
2. **Turnkey Stripe Billing Built-In:** Fully wired Stripe checkout engine with pre-configured Starter ($99/mo) and Pro ($249/mo) tiers. Price IDs are auto-provisioned directly into the buyer's Stripe account upon checkout.
3. **Multi-Tenant White-Label Architecture:** Features a modular tenant configuration (`tenant.config.ts`) allowing the platform to be white-labeled for individual contractor brands, material supply houses, or franchise networks.
4. **Automated Takeoff Engine:** Includes a proprietary material estimation module (`takeoffEngine.ts` and `fieldPricingDefaults.ts`) that helps contractors calculate rough material quantities directly from customer specs.
5. **Live & Operating:** Deployed 24/7 on Render with production SSL, rate limiting, and automated image processing pipelines.

### Included in the Sale:
- Full Intellectual Property & complete source code repository
- Modern Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, Vite 8, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion animations
- Backend API Proxy: Node.js, Express, Rate Limiters, CORS guards
- AI Integration: Server-side Google Gemini Vision API (`@google/genai`) with secure key isolation
- Automated Lead Routing: Quote request generator (`/api/quote-request`) with Resend transactional email integration
- Material Catalog: Pre-seeded CertainTeed siding and exterior color swatch database
- Deployment & Hosting scripts for Render, Firebase, and Docker
- 14 Days of asynchronous technical onboarding & architecture handover

### Immediate Monetization for the Buyer:
- **Direct Contractor Subscriptions:** Sell directly to local roofers, siding installers, and general contractors at $99–$249/mo.
- **Enterprise Licensing:** White-label the platform to regional building supply distributors or window/siding manufacturing networks.
- **Contractor Lead Generation:** Offer the tool free on a high-traffic regional site and monetize the generated homeowner quote leads.

### Reason for Sale:
Original developer looking to hand over the production asset to an operator, sales agency, or construction tech team with distribution into the home improvement trade.
